The honest check

How to know you actually need an AI agent

Forget the hype for a minute. Read these slowly and count how many sound like your normal week.

⏳ Your time is leaking

  • You finish a meeting, then spend the evening typing up what was said.
  • Proposals sit in your drafts for days while the client cools off.
  • You answer the same questions in DMs and email, one at a time.

💸 Your money is leaking

  • You pay people mainly to move information: schedules, follow-ups, invoices.
  • Work a machine could do at 2am is costing you a real salary every month.
  • You're paying for hours, but you're buying outcomes.

🔋 Your energy is leaking

  • You end every day tired, yet the work you're actually good at barely moved.
  • You are the CEO, the admin, the scheduler and customer support.
  • You can't take a day off without the queue backing up.

📈 You're leaving revenue on the table

  • Leads message you, wait hours, and buy from whoever answered first.
  • "Let me think about it" goes nowhere, because following up is manual.
  • Nobody qualifies your leads, so you spend hours on people who were never buying.

🚧 You're the bottleneck

  • Nothing in the business moves unless you personally touch it. Every task is in a queue, waiting for your attention.
Counted two or more? Then hear this: you don't need more hours, and you don't need to "work smarter." You need staff that never sleeps, never forgets to follow up, and never sends anything late. That is what an AI agent is — and it's what you'll build with us.
